About Me

I am a third-year M.S. student in Electronic Information at the Peking University National Biomedical Imaging Center, and currently a visiting researcher in the Rehg Lab at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, advised by Prof. James M. Rehg. Before that, I received my B.Eng. in Computer Science from Lanzhou University, ranking 1st of 168.

My research asks how machines can understand and anticipate human behavior from what they see. Recently I have been building world models of human gaze in video, and evaluating whether AI agents can faithfully simulate human cognition at different developmental ages. I have also worked on post-training multimodal LLMs with agentic reinforcement learning at Tencent, and on physics-constrained inverse problems for transcranial photoacoustic imaging.

Publications

Gaze World Model
Jiawen Zhang, et al.
Under review at International Conference on Learning Representations (ICLR), 2027
Treats person-specific gaze in third-person video as world prediction, unifying gaze-point estimation, gaze-shift detection and forecasting of future gaze targets. A latent predictor on a frozen V-JEPA 2.1 encoder lifts shift-detection AP from 78.4 to 89.7 over Gaze-LLE, PaGE, SAGE and frontier MLLMs.
OpenReview
Evaluating Cognitive Age Alignment in Interactive AI Agents
Jiawen Zhang, Yifan Shen, Jian Xu, Junho Kim, Ismini Lourentzou, Xu Cao, Meihuan Huang
arXiv preprint arXiv:2605.17894, 2026
ChildAgentEval: a WISC-inspired interactive web benchmark measuring whether multimodal LLM agents behave like children of a given age, with skill-guided distillation of developmental markers into agent constraints.
arXiv
Radon–Full-Waveform Inversion for Suppressing Scalp Reverberation and Skull-Induced Aberration in Transcranial Photoacoustic Computed Tomography
Jiawen Zhang, Han Yang, Jintao Ma, Bangxu Fan, Tong Shi, Xiaoyan Zheng, Songqing Xie, Zhuojun Xie, Shuai Na†
Photoacoustics, 2025Journal
A dual-domain framework where Radon-domain masks separate scalp multiples and regularize elastic FWI, improving axial resolution from 1.14 mm to 0.28 mm and SSIM from 0.42 to 0.88.
Paper
Dual-Modality US–PA Aberration Correction for Transcranial Imaging
Jiawen Zhang, et al.
Under review at IEEE Transactions on Medical Imaging (TMI), 2026
UMI-PAI estimates skull-induced phase distortion from the ultrasound reflection matrix and reuses it to correct photoacoustic reconstruction, with up to 44% PSNR gain and no CT/MR priors.

† Corresponding author
