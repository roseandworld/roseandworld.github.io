About Me

I am a third-year M.S. student in Electronic Information at the Peking University National Biomedical Imaging Center, and currently a visiting researcher in the Rehg Lab at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, advised by Prof. Jim Rehg and Prof. Ismini Lourentzou. Before that, I received my B.Eng. in Computer Science from Lanzhou University, ranking 1st of 168.

My research asks how machines can understand and anticipate human behavior from what they see. Recently I have been building world models of human gaze in video, and evaluating whether AI agents can faithfully simulate human cognition at different developmental ages. I have also worked on post-training multimodal LLMs with agentic reinforcement learning at Tencent, and on physics-constrained inverse problems for transcranial photoacoustic imaging.

Gaze World Model
Jiawen Zhang, et al.
Under review at IEEE/CVF Conference on Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition (CVPR), 2027
Treats person-specific gaze in third-person video as world prediction, unifying gaze-point estimation, gaze-shift detection and forecasting of future gaze targets. A latent predictor on a frozen V-JEPA 2.1 encoder lifts shift-detection AP from 78.4 to 89.7 over Gaze-LLE, PaGE, SAGE and frontier MLLMs.

Photoacoustics

Jiawen Zhang, Han Yang, Jintao Ma, Bangxu Fan, Tong Shi, Xiaoyan Zheng, Songqing Xie, Zhuojun Xie, Shuai Na†
Photoacoustics, 2025Journal
